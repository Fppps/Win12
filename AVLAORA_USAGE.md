# Avlaora Trademark & Brand Usage Policy

This document explains how the **Avlaora™** name, branding, logos, project identities, and related brand materials may be used.

Avlaora is currently claimed as a trademark using the `™` symbol.

Avlaora is **not being represented as a federally registered trademark**, and the `®` symbol should not be used for Avlaora unless that changes in the future.

This document may change as Avlaora grows.

---

# 1. What is Avlaora?

Avlaora is the name and brand used for projects, software, websites, concepts, experiments, media, and other work created under the Avlaora identity.

Projects may use Avlaora branding directly or may have their own names while still being associated with Avlaora.

Examples may include projects such as:

- Avlaora X
- Avlaora 12 / WebWindows 12
- VilotGRI, if officially associated with Avlaora
- Future Avlaora software, websites, tools, experiments, or projects

Not every project made by the creator of Avlaora is automatically an Avlaora project.

A project should only be treated as officially associated with Avlaora when it is explicitly presented that way.

---

# 2. The Avlaora Name

The name **Avlaora** may be mentioned normally.

You may use the name when:

- Talking about Avlaora.
- Reviewing an Avlaora project.
- Making videos about Avlaora.
- Writing articles or posts about Avlaora.
- Comparing Avlaora with another project.
- Discussing Avlaora software or development.
- Making fan content.
- Criticizing Avlaora.
- Making jokes, memes, parody, or satire involving Avlaora.
- Linking to an official Avlaora project.
- Explaining that your project works with or references Avlaora.

Normal discussion of Avlaora does not require permission.

---

# 3. Do Not Pretend To Be Avlaora

You may not present yourself, your account, your project, your organization, or your software as officially being Avlaora when it is not.

Do not:

- Claim to be the official Avlaora account.
- Claim that you created Avlaora if you did not.
- Pretend to officially represent Avlaora.
- Create fake official Avlaora announcements.
- Present unofficial software as an official Avlaora release.
- Imply that Avlaora endorsed something when it did not.
- Use Avlaora branding specifically to trick people into believing something is official.
- Create an account, website, or project designed to impersonate Avlaora.

Unofficial projects should be clearly identifiable as unofficial when confusion is reasonably possible.

---

# 4. Avlaora Project Names

Do not name an unrelated project in a way that makes it appear to be an official Avlaora product.

Examples of names that may create confusion include:

- Avlaora OS
- Avlaora Browser
- Avlaora AI
- Avlaora Studio
- Avlaora Update
- Avlaora Official
- Avlaora Corporation

unless the project is actually an official Avlaora project or permission has been given.

This does not prohibit normal descriptive references such as:

- "Theme for Avlaora X"
- "Avlaora X fan concept"
- "Unofficial Avlaora project"
- "My review of Avlaora"
- "Inspired by Avlaora"

The important difference is whether someone could reasonably believe the project came from Avlaora itself.

---

# 5. Avlaora Logos

Official Avlaora logos may be used when reasonably necessary to identify or discuss Avlaora.

Examples include:

- Reviews.
- Videos.
- News posts.
- Articles.
- Comparisons.
- Screenshots.
- Fan pages.
- Documentation referring to Avlaora.
- Project compatibility information.

Do not use an Avlaora logo in a way that falsely suggests official ownership, sponsorship, approval, or endorsement.

Do not claim an official Avlaora logo as your own work.

Do not sell the Avlaora logo itself as if it were your own brand.

---

# 6. Modified Avlaora Logos

Modified versions of Avlaora logos may be used for parody, fan content, experimentation, or commentary when they are clearly unofficial.

A modified logo should not be presented as an official replacement or redesign unless Avlaora has actually adopted it.

If a modification could easily be mistaken for an official logo, clearly identify it as unofficial.

---

# 7. Fan Content

Fan-made Avlaora content is allowed.

This may include:

- Fan art.
- Concepts.
- Mockups.
- Videos.
- Wallpapers.
- Themes.
- Memes.
- Parodies.
- Fan-made interfaces.
- Unofficial redesigns.
- Comparisons.
- Community projects.

Fan content should not deliberately misrepresent itself as an official Avlaora release.

Credit is appreciated when official Avlaora material is directly used.

---

# 8. Criticism, Reviews, Jokes & Parody

You are allowed to criticize Avlaora.

You are allowed to make jokes about Avlaora.

You are allowed to make parody or satire involving Avlaora.

You are allowed to say that you dislike Avlaora, an Avlaora project, its design, its development decisions, or anything else about it.

This policy is intended to protect the identity of Avlaora, not prevent people from having opinions.

However, do not falsely present your own statements as official Avlaora statements.

Do not fabricate fake official announcements in a way intended to deceive people.

---

# 9. Screenshots & Videos

Screenshots and recordings of Avlaora projects may generally be shared.

You may:

- Upload screenshots.
- Make videos.
- Stream Avlaora projects.
- Review builds.
- Compare builds.
- Discuss development.
- Show features.
- Archive historical screenshots.
- Make tutorials.

You do not need to pretend that Avlaora content is secret unless a particular build or file has specifically been identified as private or restricted.

---

# 10. Avlaora Software & Builds

The Avlaora trademark policy and the software license are not automatically the same thing.

Permission to mention or display the Avlaora name does not automatically give permission to:

- Redistribute source code.
- Redistribute complete builds.
- Sell Avlaora software.
- Remove copyright notices.
- Claim Avlaora code as your own.
- Repackage an Avlaora project as your own product.

Software-specific permissions may be described separately in:

- A repository LICENSE file.
- A README.
- Release documentation.
- Source-code notices.
- Another project-specific policy.

If no separate software license grants permission, do not assume that the source code is unrestricted simply because it is publicly viewable.

---

# 11. Modified Builds

If modification of a particular Avlaora project is permitted, modified versions should not be represented as official builds unless Avlaora specifically approves them.

A modified build should clearly say that it is modified or unofficial when confusion is possible.

Examples:

- "Unofficial modified Avlaora X build"
- "WebWindows 12 community modification"
- "Fan-made Avlaora concept"

Do not change an Avlaora project and then present the modified version as if Avlaora officially released it.

---

# 12. Redistribution

Redistribution rules may differ between Avlaora projects.

Unless a project's license or documentation explicitly allows redistribution, ask before redistributing complete Avlaora packages, builds, source collections, or branded releases.

Sharing a link to the official download or repository is generally preferred over re-uploading an entire official release somewhere else.

---

# 13. Commercial Use

Do not use the Avlaora name or official branding as the main identity of a commercial product or business without permission.

Do not sell products in a way that implies they are official Avlaora merchandise when they are not.

Do not use Avlaora branding to falsely advertise sponsorship, partnership, certification, or endorsement.

Normal monetized discussion may be treated differently.

For example, a monetized YouTube review of Avlaora is not the same thing as selling a product called "Avlaora OS."

---

# 14. Credit

When directly redistributing or reusing official Avlaora material where such reuse is permitted, do not remove existing attribution or ownership notices.

A simple credit may use wording such as:

`Avlaora is created and maintained by its original creator.`

or:

`Avlaora is a trademark claimed by the Avlaora project.`

The exact credit required may depend on the project or material being used.

---

# 15. The ™ Symbol

The `™` symbol may appear with the Avlaora name to indicate that Avlaora is being claimed as a trademark.

Example:

**Avlaora™**

The trademark symbol does not need to appear every single time the word Avlaora is written.

Normal writing may simply use:

**Avlaora**

Do not use:

**Avlaora®**

unless Avlaora later becomes eligible to use the registered trademark symbol.

---

# 16. Official vs. Unofficial

Something should only be described as "official Avlaora" if it actually comes from an official Avlaora source.

Unofficial projects are welcome when permitted, but they should not deliberately blur that distinction.

When necessary, use words such as:

- Unofficial
- Fan-made
- Community-made
- Concept
- Modification
- Inspired by Avlaora

to avoid confusion.

---

# 17. Compatibility Statements

It is fine to truthfully say that something:

- Works with Avlaora.
- Supports Avlaora.
- Was made for Avlaora.
- Was inspired by Avlaora.
- Uses an Avlaora project.

That does not automatically mean Avlaora endorses it.

Avoid branding that implies an official partnership when none exists.

---

# 18. Project Abandonment or Archival

An Avlaora project being discontinued, archived, replaced, or no longer actively developed does not automatically make its branding public property.

Historical Avlaora names, logos, builds, and project identities may remain associated with Avlaora even after active development ends.

Archive status does not mean "anyone can now claim this as theirs."

---

# 19. Old Branding

Older Avlaora branding may remain part of the project's history.

An old logo becoming outdated does not automatically mean somebody else can claim the logo or associated branding as their own creation.

Old logos may still be shown for historical, archival, comparison, or documentation purposes.

---

# 20. False Ownership Claims

Do not claim authorship of Avlaora branding, logos, projects, or original materials that you did not create.

Do not remove creator attribution specifically to make something appear to be your own original Avlaora project.

---

# 21. AI-Generated Material

Content generated using AI tools is not automatically official Avlaora content.

An AI-generated logo, concept, description, interface, or project should not be presented as officially approved by Avlaora unless it actually was approved or adopted.

The use of AI in the creation of an Avlaora project does not automatically transfer ownership of the Avlaora brand to the AI provider, model, or user who generated unrelated material.

---

# 22. Security & Malicious Use

Do not use Avlaora branding to distribute malware, scams, phishing pages, credential stealers, malicious downloads, or deceptive software.

Do not create fake Avlaora download pages intended to trick users into downloading harmful files.

Security researchers may discuss security issues involving Avlaora normally.

Responsible reporting of actual vulnerabilities is allowed.

---

# 23. Impersonation

Impersonation is one of the main things this policy is intended to prevent.

Do not deliberately make:

- Fake Avlaora GitHub organizations.
- Fake official Avlaora websites.
- Fake official social accounts.
- Fake release pages.
- Fake developer announcements.
- Fake update packages.

with the intention of convincing users that they are legitimate Avlaora sources.

---

# 24. Similar Names

Independent use of unrelated names is not automatically prohibited simply because they vaguely resemble Avlaora.

The concern is primarily whether branding creates realistic confusion about whether something comes from or is associated with Avlaora.

---

# 25. Permission

If you are unsure whether a particular use is allowed, you may ask for permission.

Permission granted for one specific use does not automatically grant unlimited permission for every future use.

---

# 26. Exceptions

Avlaora may explicitly approve uses that would otherwise fall outside these general rules.

An approved exception does not automatically change the rules for everyone else.

---

# 27. Updates To This Policy

This document may be updated as Avlaora changes.

New projects, branding, logos, distribution methods, or other circumstances may require additional rules or clarification.

Older versions of this document may remain available for historical purposes.

---

# 28. Trademark Registration

Avlaora currently uses the `™` designation as a claimed trademark.

This document does not state or imply that Avlaora is federally registered.

Formal registration may be pursued in the future.

Until then, Avlaora should not be represented using the `®` symbol.

---

# 29. Copyright

Trademark and copyright are different.

This document primarily concerns the **Avlaora brand identity**.

Original Avlaora works such as:

- Artwork.
- Logos.
- Source code.
- Documentation.
- Graphics.
- Videos.
- Wallpapers.
- Written material.
- Other original creative work.

may also be protected separately by copyright.

Permission to use the Avlaora name does not automatically give permission to copy every Avlaora-created work.

---

# 30. Software Licensing

This document is not automatically a software license.

Individual Avlaora projects may have their own licensing rules.

Always check the documentation of the specific project before assuming that modification, redistribution, commercial use, or source-code reuse is allowed.

---

# 31. The Main Rule

The simplest version of this entire document is:

**You may talk about, review, criticize, joke about, reference, and create fan content involving Avlaora.**

**Do not intentionally make people believe that you, your project, or your content is officially Avlaora when it is not.**

**Do not claim official Avlaora work as your own.**

That's basically it.

# Questions & Discussions

Questions about Avlaora trademark or brand usage should be brought up through the appropriate official Avlaora GitHub Discussion.

This is preferred over private contact so that questions, clarifications, and answers can remain visible to the community when useful.

If a specific use requires permission, ask about it in a Discussion before assuming permission has been granted.
